Unit 408 hallway: three directions (A, B, C)

Three hallway directions for Unit 408 around one fixed idea: the owner's LEGO Art World Map 31203 hung at true scale (104 x 65 cm, bottom edge at 1.1 m) on the plain wall over a solid floor-standing cabinet with six drawers whose top holds photos and keepsakes. A Black Marble Archive (black lacquer, marble top, brass, jute, an LED halo behind the map for the night), B Chalk and Linen (white, moulded paper pulp, natural-and-black cotton, the quietest and cheapest build) and C Honey and Ink (summer-oak mid-century on splayed legs, an anthracite lamp, a terracotta-and-ink rug). Fixed in all three: both arches dark, the honey-amber parquet, Davies Beige blackout and voile with valance boards on the window run, no hallway aircon. Nothing in the hallway is measured: conditional fit; hallway unmeasured.

A comparison for one chosen scheme. Quantities and budgets apply to that scheme. Star (☆) any piece in the schedules to collect it under Picks. The LEGO map in every map-wall render was measured against its true size: see the LEGO scale check.

The fixed brief

What every scheme must satisfy

  1. 01

    Three distinct hallway sets with the same wall composition: the owner's LEGO Art World Map 31203 at true scale (1040 x 650, bottom at 1100) over a solid floor-standing cabinet with six or more drawers and a display top. No model or family repeats across the schemes, the parallel Astra hallway set (walnut gallery, pale floating oak, oxblood atelier) or the other rooms' claims.

  2. 02

    Conditional fit; hallway unmeasured: the ceiling, the corridor width and length, both arches, the window run and the light switch position are accepted assumptions (ASSUMED.md); every plan and view depends on them until the tape numbers arrive.

  3. 03

    The cabinet, lamp, rug and plant are real catalogue products with a supplier photo, price and source link; the map is owner-supplied at nil and the LED halo (scheme A) is an electrical allowance, not a product.

  4. 04

    Kept in every scheme: both arches dark, the parquet throughout, Davies Beige blackout + voile with valance boards on the window run (Seoul Blinds offer, priced in the curtain quote, not here), no hallway aircon, no added ceiling light.

Before ordering

Resolve these procurement gates

  • Prices are frozen 20 Oct 2026 catalogue references with each line's own price date. Product existence and photos are verified; exact Cebu stock, address delivery and checkout totals are not.
  • No scheme is purchase-ready until the hallway is taped: the cabinet length against the light switch position and the corridor length, the cabinet depth against the passage width, and the map's hanging height against the cabinet top.
  • Pieces flagged "dims unpublished" or "partial" in the room placement report (the lamps, the plants, the rug depths) keep the layout size; measure them at the showroom before ordering.
  • Style renders are AI interpretations painted over the dimension-led model views; the supplier photo boards, the plan and the item schedule are the acceptance basis.
