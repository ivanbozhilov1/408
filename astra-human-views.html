How to read these views

The camera guides use the existing room drawing, selected furniture footprints and a 1.6 m eye height. The doorway view looks through the actual opening; adjacent hallway standing clearance has not been surveyed. The old window AC stays hidden behind the curtain. The split AC is on the opposite side and is not the third camera location.

These AI illustrations have been visually reviewed, but they do not certify exact image geometry, product dimensions, solar direction or installation fit. Supplier photos and measurements govern purchases. A/B bed sizes and some object heights remain planning assumptions. Existing procurement, availability and textile-fidelity caveats remain in each design’s source schedule.

Night views keep the ceiling-panel lighting on alongside the bedside lamps and valance light. Off-frame fixtures are not added to the image; their exact positions and light output remain unmeasured.
