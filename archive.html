THE CONSTANTS

The actual room
stays the same.

Ivory walls, warm short-block parquet, the fixed left wardrobe, two existing doors and the irregular native footprint. The old window AC stays concealed behind opaque Davies Beige curtains with separate white voile.

The new studies retain installed ceiling fixtures. In the revised P/Q/R night views, the existing circular white ceiling panels are on, alongside the selected bedside lamps at 2700K and the approved downward valance wash at 3000K. The specified mattress is 1828.8 × 1981.2 × 50 mm on continuous flat support.

Main views use a disclosed virtual cutaway with the camera 1175 mm beyond the south wall. The room has not been enlarged. Drawings and visuals do not certify custom support, actual bed opening, installation clearances or current Cebu stock.
