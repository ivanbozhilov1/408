Master bedroom with the queen Japanese bed

Four bird's-eye layouts for the Unit 408 master bedroom with the Scandi Home Japanese Bed in queen. The queen frame is 1500 × 2060 mm: 490 mm narrower and 80 mm shorter than the king in the current renders. That frees room for the Eames lounge chair and ottoman, a vanity for Isah, or both. Every plan is drawn to scale on the measured room and checked for walkways, door swings, curtains and the wardrobe front.
